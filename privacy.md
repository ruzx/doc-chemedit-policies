# Privacy Policy for ChemEdit
**Last updated:** November 2026

ChemEdit is a Google Docs add-on designed for drawing and editing chemical structures.

**Data Collection and Storage:** ChemEdit operates entirely within your Google Doc. We do not operate any backend servers, and we do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data, document content, or drawn chemical structures to any proprietary servers. All drawing data is stored directly within the alt-text of the generated images inside your document.

**Third-Party Services:** If you use the "Insert from Name" feature, the chemical name you type is sent securely to public chemical databases (OPSIN or PubChem) to retrieve the corresponding SMILES string. No other document content is transmitted.

**Permissions:** ChemEdit only requests permission to view and manage the specific Google Doc where the add-on is actively running (using the `@OnlyCurrentDoc` scope). It cannot read your other Google Drive files.
